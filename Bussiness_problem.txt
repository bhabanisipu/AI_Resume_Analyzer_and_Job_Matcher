Business Problem – AI Resume Analyzer and Job Matcher
Many companies receive hundreds or thousands of resumes for a single job opening. Manually reviewing every resume takes significant time and effort for HR recruiters.

At the same time, job seekers often struggle to understand whether their resumes match a particular job description, which required skills they are missing, and how they can improve their resumes.

The business problem is that there is no simple and efficient way to compare resumes with job requirements, identify skill gaps, and provide personalized improvement suggestions.
